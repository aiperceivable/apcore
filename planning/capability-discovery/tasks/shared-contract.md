# Shared Contract and Conformance

The approved contract is recorded in the protocol draft revision, revision history, changelog, decision register, and shared fixtures. Existing management manifest anchors and schemas remain available.

Use the existing root conformance format and inventory checks. Define cases for an authenticated role grant and denial, an unavailable argument or custom condition, compound conditions, an uncertain deny overriding a later grant, a definite human approval requirement, a conditional approval, and a configured final-input evaluator. Include names that are hidden, inactive, absent, moved to a new generation, and injected by a search provider.

Specify observable behavior for scope-bound cursors, deterministic ordering and normalized filters, zero disclosure for invalid cursors and unapproved IDs, and absence of normal execution effects. Cover both L1 omission of schemas and L2 return of the complete versioned schemas. Exercise the separate manifest search module and compatibility of the existing management manifest calls.
